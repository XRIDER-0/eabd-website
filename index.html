ABOUT EABD

Start with the person. Then explore the possibilities.

Empowering Ability Beyond Disability (EABD) is a Nigeria-focused initiative being developed to explore a more person-led approach to skills and opportunity for young people with disabilities.

Rather than beginning with a predetermined programme, EABD begins by asking what a person is interested in, what they can do, what they want to achieve, and what support could help them participate.

01

Discover

Discover interests, abilities, goals, experiences, and support needs.

02

Explore

Explore different possibilities and pathways based on what the person wants to pursue.

03

Develop

Build relevant skills around the person's interests, goals, and support needs.

04

Connect

Connect skills and goals with possible opportunities and next steps.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Access to training is only part of the journey.

Research in Nigeria has documented barriers affecting young people with disabilities, including limited skills and employment opportunities, accessibility barriers, financial constraints, the cost of assistive products, and social attitudes.

Research has also highlighted a gap between the aspirations of young people with disabilities and the opportunities available to them.

See What EABD Is Exploring

36.7%

Labour-force participation among people with disabilities in Nigeria, compared with 80.0% among people without disabilities.

35.7%

Employment-to-population ratio among people with disabilities, compared with 76.6% among people without disabilities.

50.5%

NEET rate among people with disabilities aged 15–24, compared with 12.3% among those without disabilities.

OUR APPROACH

The pathway begins with discovery.

EABD is developing and testing a model that starts with the individual before deciding what skills or pathway may fit.

01

Discover

What interests the person? What are their abilities, goals, experiences, and support needs?

02

Explore

Explore possible directions and pathways connected to their interests and goals.

03

Develop

Develop relevant skills through an accessible and person-centred approach.

04

Connect

Connect skills and goals to possible employment, enterprise, freelancing, education, or other opportunities.

OUR DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Disability informs the accommodation. Interest informs the pathway.

PATHWAYS TO EXPLORE

Interests can open different pathways.

These are proposed pathways within the EABD model. They are not fixed programmes or categories that determine what someone should pursue.

01

Creative Skills

Explore creative interests and skills that may lead toward freelancing, enterprise, further learning, or other opportunities.

02

Communication Skills

Explore communication-related skills that can support participation, learning, work, and everyday life.

03

Vocational Skills

Explore practical skills connected to individual interests and potential livelihood pathways.

04

Digital Skills

Explore digital skills that may support education, employment, freelancing, entrepreneurship, and participation.

WHO WE ARE BUILDING FOR

Young people with disabilities, aged 13–35.

People are not defined by a diagnosis, impairment, or predefined skill category. They have different interests, abilities, goals, experiences, and support needs.

EABD is exploring how those individual differences can shape a more relevant pathway toward skills and opportunity.

Interests

What is the person curious about?

Abilities

What can the person build on?

Goals

What does the person want to achieve?

Support

What could make participation easier?

WHERE WE ARE NOW

EABD is currently in the pre-pilot stage.

We are researching, listening, refining the model, and preparing to test what works with the people and organisations closest to the problem.

We are building with people, not just for people.

The next stage is about learning from young people with disabilities, families, organisations, practitioners, educators, employers, and other stakeholders before scaling the model.
